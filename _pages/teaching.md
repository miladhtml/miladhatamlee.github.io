My teaching connects fundamental principles with worked examples, computational tools, and engineering applications. I emphasize clear physical reasoning, explicit modeling assumptions, and careful interpretation of results.

Held full instructional responsibility for a required undergraduate course serving more than 40 students, including the syllabus, lectures, assessments, recitations, grading policy, and final grades. Topics included the first and second laws, property relations, control-volume analysis, and thermodynamic cycles.
