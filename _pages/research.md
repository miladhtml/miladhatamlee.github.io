My research uses molecular dynamics, Monte Carlo simulation, lattice dynamics, and thermodynamic modeling to connect molecular structure and interactions with measurable behavior. I build controlled model systems, validate simulation results, and use regression and numerical optimization to interpret structure–property relationships and calibrate physical models.

## Confined fluids and thermodynamics

### Water and CO₂ transport in silica nanochannels

How surface chemistry changes molecular structure, slip, and apparent permeability in pressure-driven flow. I constructed modified silica channels and analyzed the transition between flow reduction and enhancement.
