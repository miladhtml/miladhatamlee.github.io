## Research experience

**Graduate Research Assistant — University at Buffalo, 2022–2026**

- Investigated confined-fluid phase equilibrium, adsorption, and pressure-driven transport using Monte Carlo and molecular dynamics simulations.
- Built automated submission and analysis workflows supporting approximately 1,000 confined-fluid simulations, completing benchmarking and data collection in seven months.
- Calibrated attraction and covolume corrections in a modified Peng–Robinson equation of state using genetic optimization against Monte Carlo reference data.
- Analyzed how confinement and surface chemistry alter molecular structure, slip, and apparent permeability relative to continuum predictions.

**Research Assistant — Imam Khomeini International University, 2018–2021**

- Studied thermal transport, interfacial heat transfer, and mechanical response in low-dimensional materials using classical molecular dynamics.
- Compared thermal conductivity across 15 target carbon-based two-dimensional materials and analyzed 15 lateral material interfaces using NEMD and lattice dynamics.
- Investigated tensile and shear response, defects, and surface termination in phosphorene and titanium carbide/nitride MXenes.
- Used vibrational analysis and regression-based correlations to interpret relationships between atomic structure and material properties.
